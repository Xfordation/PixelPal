🐱 Let PixelPal see you're around

PixelPal uses macOS's Accessibility permission to notice when you move the mouse or type, so the cat can chase your cursor, nap when you're away, and react while you work.

Your privacy is safe: PixelPal only counts input events — it never reads which keys you press, and it never sends anything off your Mac. It has no network access at all.

Without the permission the cat still lives on your desktop and all timers work — it just won't react to your activity.
